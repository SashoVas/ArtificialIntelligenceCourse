## 📝 Homeworks

Each student must submit and defend **5-6 problems of their choice** from the 8 homework assignments, with **no more than 3 problems from each part**.

**Allowed Programming Languages:** Python, C++
Support for additional programming languages is not yet decided.

**Required:** 5-6 problems total.

**Allowed Combinations:**
* 3 + 3
* 3 + 2
* 2 + 3

#### Bonus

Students who demonstrate excellent performance may, at the assistant's discretion, present **up to 2 additional problems**. Each additional problem is worth **0.5 bonus points**.

## 📝 Lecture Exams

The lecture exams are conducted **online using Safe Exam Browser**. They consist primarily of **multiple-choice questions**, with a small number of **open-ended questions**.

> **Important:** The seminar materials alone are not sufficient to achieve a full score on these exams. Some topics are discussed exclusively during the lectures and may appear on the exams.

#### Question Types

The exams may include questions such as:

* **Algorithm execution:** Determine what an algorithm would return when applied to a given graph or problem.
* **Calculations:** Calculate metrics and quantities such as **entropy**, information gain, and other relevant measures.
* **Algorithm properties:** Determine the **completeness** and **optimality** of algorithms.
* **Algorithm classification:** Identify the type or category of a given algorithm.
* **Algorithm concepts:** Explain or identify how different algorithms work.

## 📝 Course Project

The course project is **Optional**.

Students may choose the **type and topic of their project**. Before starting, the project idea must be presented to the lecturer for approval(Schedule for the presentations will be available later in moodle).

Once approved, the completed project can be **presented at the end of the semester** and will count toward the final course grade.

## 📝 Final Exam

The final exam consists of 5 open-ended questions. Students must write detailed answers to all five questions.

After submitting the written answers, each student will have a verbal examination with the professor based on the topics and answers they have provided.
